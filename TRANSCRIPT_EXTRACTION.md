### 2.2 Canonical Jelly Bean Voice Lines & Audio Identity

The Jelly Bean addresses the player affectionately as **"Mama"** or **"Papa"** and announces its needs in short, pleading voice clips. In the episode's post-credits stinger (Lines 113–115), these frantic clips are played ironically as *"the most relaxing sounds I have"* to fall asleep to:

- `"Jelly Bean! Jelly Bean! Jelly Bean hungry! Jelly Bean hungry!"` (Line 8)
- `"Mama! Mama! Feed me!"` (Line 8)
- `"Jelly Bean cold. Papa help."` (Lines 49, 115)
- `"Mama. Mama."` (Line 114)
- `"I'm so hungry."` (Line 114)
- `"I'm so sleepy. Jellybean, sleep, sleep."` (Lines 114–115)
- `"Feed me. Feed me."` (Lines 114–115)
